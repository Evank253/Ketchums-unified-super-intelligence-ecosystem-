# Governance — Ketchum's Prometheus Unified Superintelligence

## Defining Identity

**Unified Human-Governed Superintelligence Ecosystem**

A constitutionally governed, federated superintelligence with integrated cybersecurity, defensive intelligence, autonomous engineering, scientific discovery, and immutable external human authority.

## Five Foundational Dimensions

1. **Unified Superintelligence** — Intelligence families, Bilingual Natural Language Builder, Universal Solver, specialized engines working collectively within boundaries.
2. **Constitutional and Federation Governance** — Membership, permissions, responsibilities, trust, coordination, operational boundaries.
3. **Primordial Governance** — Foundational immutable rules that no system or federation process can override.
4. **Integrated Cybersecurity and Defense** — Independently protects every system, every connection, shared infrastructure, and the entire ecosystem.
5. **External Human Authority** — Remains outside the ecosystem as the ultimate source of human authorization and required ratification.

## Governing Relationship

- Primordial Rule establishes foundational restrictions.
- Constitution defines the ecosystem’s governing structure.
- Federation governs participating systems and their relationships.
- Human Authority retains external authority over authorization and ratification.
- Cybersecurity protects the entire ecosystem under those governing restrictions.
- Intelligence operates collectively within those boundaries.

The Controller coordinates; it does not become the governing authority.  
Cybersecurity protects; it cannot exempt itself from governance.  
The Federation coordinates; it cannot grant itself sovereignty.

## Defining Principle

One unified superintelligence.  
Multiple independent intelligence capabilities.  
Integrated cyber defense.  
Constitutional and federation governance.  
An immutable Primordial Rule.  
External human authority.

## Human Authority Doctrine (summary binding)

- Successful connection does not grant authority.
- Human authority is external to the spine and expressed before crossing.
- Authorization is delegated, not derived; discovery never auto-authorizes.
- Every consequential operation traces to a human decision.
- Revocation is a first-class event and a hard stop.
- Doctrine cannot be overridden by the system it governs.

## Evidence Discipline

- Capability is not evidence.
- Qualification is not authority.
- Successful translation does not prove the original interpretation was correct.
- Distinguish explicitly stated, mathematically derived, interpreted, hypothesized, and undetermined.
- Unknowns remain visible.
